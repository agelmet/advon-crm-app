What the voice does — and only this: books an appointment into the calendar, answers practical questions (hours, address, parking, prices they allow us to say, what to bring), takes a message for a callback, and tells real emergencies to call 166 / 112. It never gives medical advice. It says in the first sentence that it is the practice's digital assistant.

The same engine works for salons, restaurants, technicians — anyone who misses calls. Only the «sheet» of hours, services and rules changes.

The phone numberYes, Greek numbers work — here are the three ways.

The key idea that makes this simple: the patient never sees our number. They call the doctor's own number; only when nobody answers does the doctor's phone forward to «our» number. So all our numbers can be landlines from one area code — the one where Advon's registered address is (e.g. 21x for Athens), even for a client in Rhodes. Forwarding to any Greek landline is inside almost every Greek phone plan, so it costs the client nothing extra.

How the doctor forwards (one minute, once)

Mobile (any network)

Dial on the phone and press call:
*61*<our number># — when I don't answer
*67*<our number># — when I'm busy
To switch off: #61# / #67#. Setting it is free; the forwarded call counts as a normal call to a landline.

Office landline

Same idea, via the provider (code, app or one call to Cosmote / Vodafone / Nova: «προώθηση όταν δεν απαντώ»). If the practice has a switchboard (τηλεφωνικό κέντρο), we add our number as the «no answer» destination — 5 minutes with whoever installed it.

The calendarNo code for Google Calendar — and a plan for Zoho.

Google Calendar

Built into ElevenLabs. We connect with Google's normal «Allow» screen. The agent gets tools to check free time and create an event. Best set-up: one Advon booking account; each practice shares its calendar with it («make changes to events»), and each agent is told which calendar is theirs.

Zoho Bookings / others

Not built in. I write one small connector (check free slots · book) that the agent calls — about a day of work once, then reused for every Zoho client. Same for any booking system that has an API.

doctoranytime only

No public API. The agent takes the request (name, phone, reason, preferred day) and the practice confirms — or the summary e-mail includes their doctoranytime link. Still better than a missed call.

Two safety rules we always follow: (1) the agent gets only «check availability» and «create event» — never «edit» or «delete», because anyone who calls could otherwise move other people's appointments (ElevenLabs' own docs warn about this); (2) we give the agent today's date and Athens time at the start of every call — without it, AI books the wrong day.

How: ElevenLabs sends every finished call to a small function on our server (the same kind that already powers the CRM), which writes the e-mail and sends it with Resend — the e-mail service you already use. A message instead of a booking («please call me back») arrives the same way, marked «Callback». Later: the same line into Telegram or the CRM.

Setting up a clientMy checklist — about one working day the first time, ~45 minutes after that.

  1. Copy the template agent 5 minGreek prompt with your rules: first sentence says it's the digital assistant · faster voice (speed ~1.1) · confirms name, service, date and time once, at the end · never medical advice · emergencies → 166/112.
  2. Fill their «sheet» 15 minHours, services and durations, prices they allow, address + parking + metro, what to bring, languages — taken from the website we built, confirmed by them on Viber.
  3. Connect the calendar 10 minThey share their Google Calendar with our booking account (2 taps, screenshots ready), or we connect Zoho via the connector.
  4. Number 10 minA Greek landline number from our pool, imported into ElevenLabs and assigned to their agent.
  5. 20 test calls 30 minA hard surname, «την άλλη Τρίτη», an interruption, an English speaker, «είσαι ρομπότ;», an emergency, a full day. Nothing goes live until these are clean.
  6. They forward their phone 1 min · theirsWe send the exact code for their network.
  7. First week: I read every call 10 min/dayAnything odd is fixed the same day — they hear it from us first.

Law & GDPRFive rules, all easy to keep.

  • Say it's AI. Since 2 August 2026 the EU AI Act (Article 50) says people must be told they're talking to AI. Our first sentence does it: «…είμαι η ψηφιακή βοηθός του ιατρείου».
  • A data agreement (DPA) with every client. They are the «controller», we are the «processor». A 2-page Greek document, signed once.
  • Collect the minimum. Name, phone, the slot, a short reason only if the caller offers it. Recordings off (or deleted after 30 days), transcripts deleted after 90 days.
  • No medical advice, ever. Emergencies → 166 / 112.
  • The robot only answers. Never automated sales calls to leads — Greek law (Ν. 3471/2006) and the «Μητρώο 11» list forbid it.

Your worries → answersWhat you told me — answered.

I don't know how to connect Twilio, or if Greek numbers even work.
They work. Twilio sells Greek local numbers; the only paperwork is a Greek address in the number's area plus a document. ElevenLabs then connects to Twilio with two codes — it's a built-in feature, not code. I do every click; you only upload the document once.
There are already many Greek companies selling this.
True — and they all use similar engines. We don't win on technology; we win because we already look after the client's website and they trust you. We sell it inside the package, priced by minutes so it always profits.
Salons use 20–60 minutes a day — won't that kill the margin?
Not with minute-based tiers: a 1,800-minute salon goes on Pro + extra minutes. Every tier leaves 55–70% with us.
What if it books the wrong day or mishears a name?
It confirms everything once at the end, it gets today's date on every call, it can't edit or delete appointments, and I read every call for the first week. If it's unsure, it takes a message instead of booking.
What if it breaks?
If the agent doesn't answer, the call falls back to the practice's voicemail like today — the patient is never stuck. And every client's calls are checked daily at the start.
Will patients hang up on a robot?
Some will — so it says who it is immediately and offers «να σας καλέσει ο γιατρός;». Today they hang up on silence. We'll measure it in the pilot.
Is it legal?
Yes, with the five rules above: say it's AI, sign a DPA, collect the minimum, no medical advice, never cold-call with it.
