How an owner thinksFive rules that separate “a person who works” from “a person who owns a business”.

Every business you’ve seen running ads in the US or anywhere else has no secret. It has these five rules in the owner’s head. Read them as if someone who has done it many times is telling you — because that’s how they’re written.

1 · You are not the business. You are its first investor.

Your job is not to do the work. It is to decide where every euro and every hour goes so that more comes back. When you build a site or answer a chat, you work FOR the business. When you decide “€1,000 into ads this month”, you work ON the business. Only the second one makes it bigger.

2 · Every euro is either a cost or an investment. Never both.

Cost = goes out and brings nothing back (Netlify credits for a tiny change). You are right to be stingy there. Investment = €1 goes out and €2 comes back (an ad that brings a meeting for less than the meeting is worth). There the owner does the opposite: looks for ways to put in MORE. The one-person mistake is treating both the same way — as a cost.

3 · Measure the ratio, then feed the machine.

A machine is a ratio: X in, Y out. First you measure it with little money (€300). If €1 → €2, you put in every euro you can find, because every euro you DON’T put in is lost profit. If €1 → €0.70, you don’t “spend more and hope” — you fix the machine (ad, page, offer) and measure again. You never scale something you haven’t measured.

4 · Buy back your time.

Your hour is worth the BEST thing you could do in it. If one hour of meetings brings 2 clients, your hour is worth hundreds of euros (the exact number is below). Every task someone would do for €15 an hour — changes, chasing payments, chats — costs you the difference each time you do it yourself. “I’ve never hired” is a fear, not a reason. We solve it below.

5 · One experiment at a time, with a budget and a stop line.

Owners don’t “take risks”. They set: this much money, this many days, this number means “continue”, this number means “stop”. Then they decide with the number, not the feeling. That way a “failure” costs €300 and a lesson, never the business. And a “success” becomes an instant order: double it.

The only thing you are truly missing: one number. What a booked meeting costs when you buy it from Meta instead of earning it on the phone. You don’t know it, so your head fills the gap with “I’m small”. Below we work out how much you CAN pay — and then the only thing left is to measure it.

Cash flow

You pay the ad today; the client pays at the first draft, 4–10 days after the meeting. So you need roughly one month of ads up front — your “few thousand” is exactly that. From month 2 the machine pays for itself.

Not on money. On your hours: look at the “Your hours” column. Once it passes ~120 hours a month, you don’t need less advertising — you need the first person (section “Hiring”). That is the good moment to hire: when the work already exists and already pays.

Hiring without fearYou don’t hire “an employee”. You buy hours, a few at a time, with a trial.

“I’ve never done it” is true for every owner the first time. The answer isn’t courage — it is making it so small that it can’t go wrong expensively. There is a ladder, and you are already on the second step.

Only when meetings exceed what you can hold: someone who takes the 15-minute meeting with your script, paid on commission (e.g. €30 per client). Their close rate will be worse than yours at first — and that is fine, because they are only paid when they close.

I don’t know how to find / choose a person.
You don’t choose — you test. Give 3 candidates the same small paid task (2 client chats + 1 change with Claude, 2 hours, €30 each). Keep the one who wrote the best Greek and asked the right questions. Claude writes the ad, the criteria and the test.
They will make mistakes with my clients.
They will. That is why in month one NOTHING goes out without your ✓ — exactly as Claude works today (prepares, you approve). Your ✓ takes 1 minute; the work you save takes an hour.
What if I then have no work for them?
That is why steps 2 and 3 are per result or part-time with a trial. You stop with one message. “Big” businesses do exactly this: freelancers first, employees later.
€1,000 a month sounds expensive.
The cost is compared to what you do with the hours. 100 hours a month × the value of your hour in meetings = see the box “Value of one hour”. The €1,000 may be the cheapest thing on this whole page.

What can go wrongThe six ways this plan fails — and how it doesn’t.

Cost per meeting comes out at double the “most”.
It often does in month 1. It doesn’t mean “ads don’t work for me” — it means ONE of the four (video, copy, page, audience) is wrong. Change one, measure again with €300. Three rounds at most.
Leads come in but don’t show up to the meeting.
Measure “meetings that happened”, never “leads”. Fix: an automatic SMS reminder 1 hour before, a calendar that asks for a phone number, and the question “do you already have a website?” on the form to filter.
You run 4 ideas at once and none of them reaches the number.
The most common failure of capable people. One machine at a time: first the website ads (the sure one), in parallel ONLY the 100 calls (€0). Θέμα Δ, Panta, vresfrontistirio wait — not because they are bad, but because they are a different business (consumers, thousands of users, support) and need ads from day one. You will know ads in 3 months.
You sell a lot of social/Google Ads and can’t deliver in time.
Social is half the “delivery” risk on this page. Fix: Claude builds every client’s month with a scheduled task on the 20th, the person checks, you approve in 10 minutes. You accept at most 10 new social clients a month for the first 3 months.
Cash: the ad is paid today, the client pays in 10 days.
Always keep one month of ads aside. Never raise the budget above 1/3 of last month’s revenue — a rule that keeps you safe without slowing you down.
Meta disables the ad account / rejects videos.
It happens to everyone. The “Advon Media” account must have a verified business and tax ID, a second admin, and always 2–3 approved creatives waiting. And Google Ads on the search “κατασκευή ιστοσελίδας δωρεάν” as a second channel from month 3, so you never hang from one.

If I were in your chair

Six lines, no detours.

  1. You stop measuring yourself by what YOU do in a day. Your business is a ratio (€ in → € out) and an hour of yours worth hundreds. Those are what you measure.
  2. You switch the website ads on THIS week at €30/day — not because you are sure, but to learn the one number you’re missing. The €300 is the price of knowing.
  3. You sell social/Google Ads to the 220 who already trust you BEFORE you look for the 221st. It is the cheapest money you will ever make.
  4. You hire the first person when your hours pass 120 — not earlier, not later. Per result, or with a 2-week trial. The fear leaves in the first week.
  5. You win the high-ticket with 100 calls and 2 pilots, THEN you feed it with ads. One client a month is enough: €3,000 + €250/month × 12 months = €36,000 a year later.
  6. One machine at a time. The rest of the Ideas page waits its turn — nothing is lost, it goes in a queue. In 90 days you come back here with real numbers and we rewrite it.
