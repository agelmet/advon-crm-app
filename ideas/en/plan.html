02 · The three truths (what I think, straight)
Truth 1

Websites ARE the business. You are right.

Hosting is recurring, near-100% margin, easy to sell and compounding: every site you build today pays again next year. And your edge (Claude builds for ~0€) will not stay unique past 2027 — so it is a land-grab now, not later.

Truth 2

Services: yes — on demand, never an agency.

A psychologist pays 10,83€/month for the site and would pay 99–190€/month for patients. You take it — but only what Claude delivers productised with ≤15 minutes of human time a month. No “account manager team” now: that eats margin and your sales team.

Truth 3

The limit is your hands, not demand.

10k of new money a month needs ~77 sites = ~170 meetings = ~1,700 calls a month. One person cannot do that; 2 callers + ads + 2 operators can, with you only in the meetings. Hiring is not “when I have time” — hiring IS the time.

Advon is a subscription company for Greek professionals: line one is hosting (yearly), line two is whatever they ask for (monthly). Both on the same clients. Everything else is a distraction.

08 · Hiring in Greece — the whole process

Who to call, what it costs, how not to get burned.

Short version: your accountant (Chiotis) does all the paperwork; you choose the person and the form of contract. Three forms, real 2026 costs, where to find people, the steps.

Form A · Employee (part- or full-time)

Best for the operator

  • Declared in ΕΡΓΑΝΗ (form Ε3) by the accountant before the first hour of work
  • Minimum wage from 1 Apr 2026: 920€ gross/month full-time (≈772€ net) · 4h/day ≈ 460€ gross
  • Employer contributions ≈ 22% on top · 14 salaries a year (bonuses + holiday)
  • All-in: full-time ≈ 1,300€/month, 4h ≈ 650€/month
  • Digital work card — accountant sets it up · payroll +30–50€/month
Form B · Freelancer (μπλοκάκι)

For callers & specialists

  • They invoice you · no ΕΡΓΑΝΗ
  • Perfect for commission callers (e.g. 40€ per meeting that shows + 60€ per closed client) and per-project specialists
  • The trap: if they work mainly for you (1–2 clients, fixed hours) the law treats it as employment and you pay the contributions (law 4387/2016 art. 39 §9). Full-time people → Form A.
Form C · Subsidised hire (ΔΥΠΑ)

Free money if the timing fits

  • 2026 programmes: unemployed 30+ → 35–75% of labour cost for 12 months · 18–29 no experience → up to 1,256€/month · mothers up to 1,004€/month
  • Condition: registered unemployed with ΔΥΠΑ before hiring · no layoffs in the previous quarter
  • Apply on the ΔΥΠΑ portal (accountant) → approval → ΕΡΓΑΝΗ. They open in waves — ask Chiotis which is open

Where to find the people

  • kariera.gr, skywalker.gr, jobfind.gr — paid ad ~100–200€ · LinkedIn free
  • University career offices (Πάντειο, ΕΚΠΑ, ΟΠΑ, ΑΠΘ) for part-time students — ideal operator: final-year marketing/communications, organised, perfect Greek
  • Facebook groups «Αγγελίες εργασίας Αθήνα», «Part-time jobs»
  • Your own clients: a message to 10 clients — psychologists know organised young people
  • Callers: people who already sold by phone (insurance, telecoms, energy) · ad with «commission + bonus»
  • Remote Greeks abroad (Germany, UK) — cheaper, no office

The process (2 weeks, ~6 hours of your time)

1
Job ad — Claude writes it: «Operations assistant at a web agency, 4h, remote/Athens, 500€ + bonus». Ask for a 1′ voice note “why you want it” (filters 80%).
2
Screen — Claude ranks CVs; you read 10, call 5 for 15′.
3
Paid trial (2 h, 30€) — reply to 3 real client messages, QA one draft on a phone, log it in the CRM.
4
Offer — send Chiotis: name, ΑΦΜ, ΑΜΚΑ, IBAN, start date, hours, salary. He files ΕΡΓΑΝΗ + contract. Start on a Monday.
5
Onboard — manual + videos by Claude; 2 weeks beside you 1 h/day; from week 3 they own support. 3-month trial period — use it.

Company form — talk to Chiotis in December

You are a sole proprietorship. With employees and net above ~40–50k€/year, an ΙΚΕ usually wins: 22% corporate tax + 5% on dividends instead of the personal scale that climbs to 44%, limited liability, and — critical for acquisitions — a company you can sell or use to buy others. Ask him for the exact crossover for your case and the cost (typically 1–2k€, 2–4 weeks).

12 · The bigger picture — exits and “private equity”

What your company is worth, and what buying companies really means at each stage.

Stage 1 · Advon at 20k net

≈ 0.7–1.2 M€

Small agencies sell for 2.5–4× annual owner earnings, more when revenue is recurring and the owner is replaceable. 950 self-renewing subscriptions + a team that runs = the top of the range. Without the team, the same revenue is worth ~1×, because the buyer is buying you.

Stage 2 · Advon at 100k net

≈ 4–7 M€

1.2 M€/year of mostly recurring profit, 2,600 subscriptions and software inside = a strategic asset for a Greek/EU marketing group or a SaaS roll-up. 4–6× EBITDA. A sellable company, not a job.

Stage 3 · You as the buyer

Entrepreneurship through acquisition

“Private equity” at this scale = buying small businesses (100k–1M€) with seller financing (the owner is paid over 3–5 years from the business’s own profit), improving them with your machine, selling at a higher multiple. Targets: ageing web agencies with 300+ hosting clients, local lead-gen businesses, directories. You buy clients, not code.

What a buyer (or a bank) will ask you in 2029

  • ΙΚΕ with clean books for 24+ months (Chiotis)
  • Recurring revenue ≥ 70% · cancellations <10%/year
  • A GM who runs it; you below 20% of operations
  • Documented systems (CLAUDE.md is literally the operations manual)
  • Client contracts (even 1 page), consent for marketing use

Why not skip to buying now

Because every acquisition needs cash flow to service the debt and a team to absorb the business. At 20k net you buy a 300k€ company with 100k down; at 100k net you buy 1–2 M€ companies. The ladder is the same: recurring revenue → replace yourself → clean entity → buy with the profit.
