Advon Pay — renewals that charge by themselves

Clients save a card once in a small portal and their hosting renews by itself every year — no more chasing renewals by hand.

On this pageWhy it can workNumbersWatch-outsNext step

Why it can workWhy it's worth it

Turns 200+ one-off yearly payments into automatic ones, cuts the chasing, and gives a place to offer extras (social, Google Ads, the receptionist).

NumbersThe numbers

200+ clients × €130/year

Watch-outsWatch out for

Clients must agree to save a card; needs a clear Greek message. Your current rule is bank transfer + τιμολόγιο only (no Stripe in client messages) — this idea would need a Greek card provider such as Viva and your explicit decision.
Next step

Decide whether you want card payments at all; if yes, compare Viva vs others, then send the onboarding message to the clients renewing next.
