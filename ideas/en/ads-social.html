Access without passwordsHow it really works — you click, they approve.

Meta (Instagram + Facebook)

Never take a password: it's against Meta's rules, a login from a new device locks the account, and if anything goes wrong the person with the password gets blamed.

  1. In Advon's Meta Business portfolio: Settings → Accounts → Pages → Add → «Request access to a Page».
  2. Type the client's page name, tick only Content and Insights.
  3. Same for Instagram accounts (and ad accounts only if we'll run Meta ads).
  4. The client presses Approve. You check «Partners» shows Advon Media.

They keep the account. Everything we post appears as their page. They remove us with one click whenever they want.

Google Ads

  1. Once: we create Advon's free Manager account (MCC) — one login (yours) that holds every client account.
  2. New client: we create their account from inside the Manager — it's linked to us from birth — and invite their Gmail as Admin. They add card + ΑΦΜ.
  3. Already has an account: they send their 10-digit Customer ID; we send a link request; they press Accept.
  4. When we stop, we unlink. The account and all its history stay theirs.

Invoices: Google Ireland bills the client directly. With their ΑΦΜ entered it's an EU business service (reverse charge, 0% VAT on the invoice) — their accountant handles it. Ask Chiotis once for the one sentence we put in our terms.

What decides if it works

  • The right words. «ψυχολόγος Περιστέρι» yes; «ψυχολόγος μισθός» no. One shared list of «negative» words (δωρεάν, δημόσιο, νοσοκομείο, σπουδές, μισθός, αγγελίες, pdf…) on every campaign.
  • The right area. A radius around the practice — only people physically there.
  • The right hours. When someone can answer the phone (or the AI receptionist can).
  • Measuring. A small tag on their site counts every tap on the call button or booking link — loaded only after the cookie «Αποδοχή», as our consent system already does. Adding it to a live site = one Netlify deploy (15 credits) — we ride it with another change when possible.
  • Patience. Weeks 1–3 are «learning». Month 2 is cheaper than month 1.

What we will NOT do

  • No Performance Max, Display banners or YouTube for small businesses — they burn money on people who weren't searching.
  • No «smart» campaigns that hide the search words.
  • No spending on your card, ever.
  • No promises of a number of patients/customers — we promise measured contacts.
  • No superlatives («ο καλύτερος», «Νο1»).

Rules that protect youThe law and the platforms, in plain words.

Doctors & dentists

The medical code (Ν. 3418/2005, άρθρο 17) forbids doctors «personal advertising or systematic public presence» and publishing thank-you letters or success stories to advertise. A website and factual information are allowed. So: for doctors and dentists, social = information (the space, the team, knowledge posts, opening hours) — never «testimonial» ads, never «best». Paid ads for doctors only as factual information, after we look at the case together. Start Google Ads with psychologists, physios, dietitians, speech therapists.

Meta's health rules (since January 2025)

Meta limits what health & wellness advertisers can track and optimise for. So: on Meta we optimise for calls, messages and landing-page views — not «purchases» — and never target people by health condition. Organic posting (what the packages are) is not affected.

Google's rules

No prescription medicines in ads, no claims you can't prove, no personalised ads based on health. Ads for services («ψυχοθεραπεία», «φυσικοθεραπεία») are normal. Google may ask the client to verify the business once.

Cookies & GDPR

The measuring tag loads only after the visitor presses «Αποδοχή» — the system we already have on every site. We never store their patients' data; reports show numbers only.

Your worries → answersEvery doubt you've told me, answered.

I've never run Google Ads.
Neither has any agency before its first client. The difference: I know the platform end to end, I do the building and the weekly checks, and the learning run on Advon means you will have run one for real before you sell it.
How much time will it take me?
About 5 minutes per client per month. Everything else — designs, captions, scheduling, ads checks, reports — is mine, run by scheduled tasks.
What do I need from them?
Nothing compulsory. For social: approve one request. For ads: accept one invite, add their card and ΑΦΜ. Photos are optional — we build from their website.
How do I get access without their passwords?
Partner access (Meta) and the Manager account (Google). They approve with one click and remove us with one click.
What if the ads don't bring calls?
The free area check stops bad cases before they start; the 60-day guarantee means no fee if we don't hit 10 contacts at the agreed budget; and we only promise contacts we can count.
What if they hate a post?
They approve the whole month before anything goes out — and change what they want until the 27th.
What if a client wants to stop?
One message before the 20th. They keep everything. We remove our access the same day.
Can I take any business, not only doctors?
Yes — use the «Which service for whom» table. Salons and restaurants are actually easier than doctors: no medical code.
