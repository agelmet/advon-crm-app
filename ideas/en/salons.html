The 3 sentences60 seconds, not a presentation.

Inside the shop · to the owner (said in Greek)
Καλημέρα! Είμαι ο Άγγελος από την Advon Media. Φτιάχνουμε τις καλύτερες ιστοσελίδες στην Ελλάδα, εντελώς δωρεάν. Έχουμε πάνω από 200 online και 130 αξιολογήσεις 5★ στο Google. Μόνο 10€ τον μήνα έξοδα — με 1 πελάτη τον χρόνο είστε σε κέρδος για πάντα. Σας αφήνω την κάρτα μου· σκανάρετε το QR να δείτε δείγματα.

Why it can workWhy it's worth one hour

You can try it any day, for one hour, with no cost beyond the cards — and you see straight away whether it brings anything. The owner is usually in (decides on the spot), most live only on Instagram with no site of their own, and clients come back every few weeks — so one new client a year pays the €130 many times over. Face to face builds trust faster than the phone.

What to carrySmall kit, big effect.

  • 100 cards with a QR code to advonmedia.com/dorean?utm_source=karta — that's how we count what they bring.
  • Your phone with 2–3 of our best sites open (a beauty one if we have it, one with nice effects, one with Google reviews).
  • Advon's Google reviews ready to show («130 reviews, 5★»).
  • A notebook or the Quick Log for every shop.

How to walk itOne street, one hour.

  1. Pick the area the night before 10 minGoogle Maps → «κομμωτήριο» + «νύχια» → a street or square with 10–15 of them close together. Prefer shops with many Google reviews and only Instagram (no website).
  2. Go Tuesday–Thursday, 11:00–13:00Not at peak hours, not Saturday.
  3. Ask for the owner«Είναι ο/η ιδιοκτήτης εδώ;» If not: leave the card and write their name and phone in the Quick Log for a call.
  4. 3 sentences, card, smile, leaveIf they ask questions, show one site on the phone. If they say yes, book the 15-minute Meet on the spot.
  5. Log every shopName, area, owner in/out, interest 1–3 — in the Quick Log, before the next door.

Watch-outsWhat not to do.

  • Don't go at peak hours — Tuesday–Thursday 11:00–13:00 is best.
  • 60 seconds, not a presentation.
  • Always say «free» together with the €10/month, so it doesn't sound suspicious.
  • Don't push — the card keeps working later.
