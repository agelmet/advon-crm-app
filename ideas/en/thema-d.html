Θέμα Δ — exam machine for Πανελλήνιες

Working name «Θέμα Δ». Not «an AI helper»: the student sits a past paper BY HAND with a 3-hour timer, photographs it, gets graded like a βαθμολογητής against the official marking scheme, sees exactly where marks were lost, and the parents get a report every Sunday. Plan + clickable MVP are built.

On this pageWhy it can workNumbersWatch-outsNext step

Why it can workWhy it's worth it

89,032 Πανελλήνιες candidates in 2026 and parents already pay for φροντιστήρια. What ChatGPT will never do: real exam conditions, grading against the official solutions, a weak-spot map, the parent report (the parent is the buyer) and a class board for the teacher. Sell THROUGH φροντιστήρια (they renew every year) and PRIVATE SCHOOLS (one contract = 100–300 students; they compete on Πανελλήνιες results and many also run IB). Start with Θετικές: Μαθηματικά, Φυσική, Χημεία. Γυμνάσιο and IB/IAL later, with the same engine.

NumbersThe numbers

89,032 candidates in 2026 (75,109 ΓΕΛ + 13,923 ΕΠΑΛ)Parents: €24.90/month or €199 for the season (Sept–June)Φροντιστήρια: €149/month up to 40 students · €249 up to 100Private schools: €30–40 per student/year, minimum €2,500AI cost ≈ €0.10–0.30 per graded exam (estimate)

Watch-outsWatch out for

Grading must be right every time: official marking scheme only, official solution shown side by side, «Διαφωνώ» button, the teacher has the final say. EU AI Act: AI that grades students = high-risk, rules apply from 2 Dec 2027. Minors: the parent opens the account (consent under 15). Seasonal: students leave every June, so φροντιστήρια and schools are the base.
Next step

1) Try «Ζωντανή διόρθωση» in the MVP with your API key on a handwritten answer. 2) Show it to a maths teacher for 1 hour. 3) Call 15 φροντιστήρια (start with the vresfrontistirio sign-ups). If 3–4 say yes: real MVP in November, free pilot in January, charging from February; private schools Feb–June for September 2027.
